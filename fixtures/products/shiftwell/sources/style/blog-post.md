# How to handle the 6am sick call without losing your morning

Every manager in a clinic or care home knows the feeling. Your phone lights up before your alarm does. Someone on the early shift isn't coming in, and the shift starts at seven.

What happens next decides how the rest of your day goes. Here are three habits that make the 6am sick call a lot less painful, whether you use Shiftwell or not.

## 1. Decide who you'll ask before you need them

The worst time to work out who could cover a shift is when you're half awake and in a hurry. You end up messaging the same three reliable people every time. They get tired of it, and one day they stop answering.

Instead, sit down once a month and write a short cover list for each role. Who's qualified? Who has said they'd like extra hours? Who lives close enough to get in at short notice?

- Keep a cover list for each role, not one for everyone
- Note who wants extra hours and who doesn't
- Share the load, so the same people aren't always asked

In Shiftwell, this list is built for you. When you offer an open shift, only people with the right role and qualifications see it. You can offer it to everyone eligible, or just to the group you choose.

## 2. Ask everyone at once, not one at a time

Ringing round one person at a time is slow. You call, you wait, you leave a voicemail, you call the next person. By the time someone says yes, the shift has started.

It's quicker to ask your whole cover list at once and take the first yes. People who are free can reply straight away. People who aren't can ignore it, with no awkward phone call.

With Shiftwell, a sick call turns the shift into an open shift. Your team gets a notification on their phones. The first eligible person to accept gets the shift, and everyone else sees it's been taken.

## 3. Make the change official straight away

Once someone agrees to cover, the job isn't done. The rota needs updating. The person who called in sick needs marking as off. And the rest of the team needs to see the right names on the shift.

If any of that waits until later, mistakes creep in. Two people turn up. Nobody turns up. Someone gets paid for a shift they didn't work.

- Update the rota the moment cover is agreed
- Record the sick call, so you can spot patterns later
- Let the team see the change, not just the person covering

In Shiftwell, this happens as part of the same step. The rota updates when someone accepts the shift. The sick call is recorded. And the change shows up on everyone's phone.

## A quick word on patterns

Once you're recording sick calls properly, you'll start to see patterns. Maybe Monday mornings are always hard. Maybe one shift pattern leaves people too tired for the next. That's useful to know, and much easier to act on than a vague feeling.

You don't have to act on all of it at once. Pick the one pattern that costs you the most mornings, and start there.

## The short version

You can't stop people getting ill. But you can stop the 6am sick call taking over your day.

- Plan your cover list before you need it
- Ask everyone eligible at once
- Make the change official straight away
