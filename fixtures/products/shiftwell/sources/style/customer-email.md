Subject: You're live on Shiftwell. Here's your first fortnight.

Hi there,

Welcome to Shiftwell. We're really glad to have you.

Your setup is done, your team is in, and your first rota is ready to go. Nothing will break if you leave it alone. But a few small steps over the next two weeks will make a big difference to how much time Shiftwell saves you.

Here's the plan we suggest.

## Week one, Monday: check your shift templates

Open Shiftwell Web and go to your templates. These are the shifts you run every week, like an early, a late and a night.

Check the times and the breaks look right. If your long day actually finishes at 20:30, not 20:00, fix it now. Every rota you build from here will be right first time.

## Week one, Wednesday: get your team on their phones

Your team will get an invite by email. Most people are set up in a couple of minutes.

A few tips that help:

- Put a note up in the staffroom with the date you're switching over
- Ask your senior on each shift to help anyone who gets stuck
- Stop sending the rota by text, so everyone has a reason to look

## Week one, Friday: publish next week's rota

Build next week from your templates and publish it. Everyone with a shift gets a notification.

This is the moment the paper rota can come off the wall. It's a good feeling.

## Week two, Monday: try an open shift

Next time someone calls in sick, don't reach for your contacts. Post the shift as an open shift instead.

Choose who should see it. Then watch the replies come in. Most managers tell us this is the moment Shiftwell clicks.

## Week two, Wednesday: set your staffing rules

Tell Shiftwell how many people each shift needs, and which roles need which qualifications. From then on, it'll warn you before you publish a rota that's short.

- Start with your minimum numbers per shift
- Add qualifications for your clinical roles
- Leave the finer rules until you've settled in

## Week two, Friday: approve your first timesheets

Your team has been clocking in for a week or so. Open timesheets, check anything that's flagged, and approve.

That's one less spreadsheet to keep.

## Need a hand?

Just reply to this email. A real person on our team reads every message, and we'll get back to you.

Some things people often ask in their first fortnight:

- How do I change someone's role? Open their profile and pick a new role.
- Can a carer work at two of our homes? Yes. Add the second location to their profile.
- What if someone loses signal? They'll still see the rota they last synced.

## One last thing

Give it a couple of weeks before you judge it. The first rota always feels new. By the third, you'll wonder how you managed without it.

All the best,

The Shiftwell team at Brightline Software
