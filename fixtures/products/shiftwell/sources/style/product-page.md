# How Shiftwell works

Shiftwell is staff scheduling for clinics and care homes. It has two parts: Shiftwell Web, where you build and manage the rota, and Shiftwell Mobile, where your team sees their shifts and picks up new ones. Together, they take the chasing out of covering shifts.

[Book a demo]

## Shiftwell Web, for you

Shiftwell Web is where you plan. Open it in your browser and you'll see the rota for each location by day, week or four-week block.

Building a rota starts with the shifts you already run. An early, a late, a long day, a night. Save each one as a template once, and drag them onto the rota from then on. Next month's rota becomes a copy of this month's with a few changes, not a blank page.

- Save your usual shifts as templates
- Copy last month's rota and adjust it
- Keep drafts private until you're ready to publish

When you're happy, publish. Everyone with a shift on that rota gets a notification. Nobody has to read it off the staffroom wall.

## Shiftwell Mobile, for your team

Shiftwell Mobile is the part your team will use every day. It's simple on purpose.

Your staff open it and see their next shifts. They can add those shifts to their own calendar, so their partner knows when they're working too. They can tell you when they're free and when they'd like time off. And when an open shift comes up that fits, they can take it with a tap.

- See upcoming shifts and leave at a glance
- Pick up open shifts that match their role
- Request swaps and leave without a phone call

If someone is on the bus with no signal, they can still see the rota they last synced. When they're back online, it catches up.

## Open shifts, sorted

This is the bit most managers come for.

Say a nurse calls in sick for tomorrow's late shift. In Shiftwell, the shift turns into an open shift. You pick who gets offered it: everyone eligible, or just your regular pool of part-timers. Shiftwell only offers it to people who have the right role, hold the right qualification and aren't already working.

They get a notification on their phone. The first person to accept gets the shift. If you'd rather confirm it yourself, switch on approval and you'll get a nudge to say yes.

## Rules that keep shifts safe

Every location runs on rules, even if they live in your head. Shiftwell lets you write them down once.

- Set minimum staff for each role on each shift
- Add the qualifications a role needs, with expiry dates
- Set weekly hour limits and rest time between shifts

When a shift breaks a rule, Shiftwell tells you before you publish. You can still go ahead if you need to, but you'll be asked for a reason. That reason goes in the record, so you're never left explaining it from memory.

## Clock-ins and timesheets

Your team clocks in on their phone or at a shared tablet near the entrance. If you like, you can ask phones to be on site when someone clocks in.

Shiftwell turns clock-ins into timesheets. You look over the week, fix anything odd, like a missed clock-out, and approve. Then you export the timesheets for whoever runs your wages.

## Works with what you already use

You don't need to type your team in by hand. Bring your staff list in from a spreadsheet, or connect your HR system on the Enterprise plan.

Shift alerts can go to your team's group channel too, so nobody misses an open shift. And if you have developers on hand, there's an API for building your own connections.

## Two plans, one way of working

Shiftwell comes on a Standard plan and an Enterprise plan. Standard covers everything a single clinic or home needs day to day. Enterprise adds the extras larger groups ask for, like single sign-on and extra integrations. Your account manager will walk you through which fits.
