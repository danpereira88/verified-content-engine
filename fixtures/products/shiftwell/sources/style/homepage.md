# Fill every gap in your rota, without ringing round

It's 6am and your phone is buzzing. A carer on the early shift is sick, and the shift starts in an hour. You know the drill: scroll through your contacts, send a dozen texts, and hope someone says yes before breakfast.

Shiftwell gives you a better morning. You post the open shift once, and the right people get it on their phones straight away. The first person who's free and qualified picks it up, and you get on with your day.

[See Shiftwell in action]

## Your whole rota, in one place

Most managers we talk to keep the rota in a spreadsheet, a paper sheet on the staffroom wall, or both. That works until someone swaps a shift and forgets to tell you.

With Shiftwell, the rota lives in one place. You build it on your laptop. Your team sees it on their phones. When something changes, everyone sees the same version.

- Build next month's rota from the shifts you already use
- Publish it to your team in one click
- See who's working, who's off and who's on leave at a glance

## Cover sick calls before the shift starts

Sick calls are part of running a clinic or a care home. They shouldn't eat your whole morning.

When someone calls in sick through Shiftwell, their shift turns into an open shift right away. You choose who gets offered it. Your team sees it on their phones, and the first person who accepts gets the shift. If you'd like to check before it's confirmed, you can.

- Offer open shifts to the whole team or a chosen group
- Only people with the right role and qualifications see them
- You stay in control of who's confirmed

## Swaps your team can sort out themselves

Your team wants to swap shifts. That's fine. What isn't fine is finding out on the day.

In Shiftwell, a swap starts with a request on the phone. The other person accepts it. Then you approve it, or you let swaps between the right people go through on their own. Either way, the rota stays up to date and you always know who's coming in.

## Safe staffing, on every shift

You know how many people each shift needs. Shiftwell knows it too, once you tell it.

Set the minimum staff for each role and each shift. If a shift drops below it, Shiftwell shows it in red so you can't miss it. Add the qualifications a role needs, and Shiftwell won't put someone on a shift their training doesn't cover.

## Clocking in that just works

Your team can clock in on their own phones or on a shared tablet by the door. Clock-ins become timesheets automatically. At the end of the week, you check them, approve them and send them on.

- Clock in on a phone or a shared tablet
- Timesheets build themselves from clock-ins
- Missed clock-outs are flagged for you to fix

## Built with care for your team's data

Your team trusts you with their details. We take that seriously.

Your data is encrypted on its way to us and while we store it. You decide who can see what, with simple roles for staff, schedulers and admins. And every change to the rota is recorded, so you can always see who changed what.

## Questions you might have

### Will my team need training?

A little. Most people pick up the phone side in a few minutes. We run a training session for your schedulers during setup.

### Do my staff need new phones?

Usually not. Shiftwell works on most iPhones and Android phones from the last few years.

### Can I use it across more than one site?

Yes. Shiftwell is built for teams that work across several clinics or homes. Staff can belong to more than one location.
