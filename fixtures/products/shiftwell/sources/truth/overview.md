# Shiftwell overview

Shiftwell is a staff-scheduling and shift-management service made by Brightline Software. Shiftwell combines Shiftwell Web for managers and Shiftwell Mobile for staff. Shiftwell is used to build rotas, fill open shifts, manage shift swaps and record worked hours.

## Who Shiftwell is for

Shiftwell is designed for outpatient clinics, care homes and community care teams. Typical Shiftwell users are clinic managers, care-home managers, rota coordinators and frontline care staff. Shiftwell supports day shifts, night shifts, split shifts and on-call shifts.

## Organizations and locations

An organization in Shiftwell contains one or more locations. An organization on the Standard plan can contain up to 25 locations. An organization on the Enterprise plan can contain an unlimited number of locations. Each Shiftwell location supports up to 200 staff profiles. A staff profile can be assigned to up to 5 locations in the same organization.

## Components

### Shiftwell Web

Shiftwell Web is the browser dashboard for managers and rota coordinators. Shiftwell Web supports the current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. Shiftwell Web shows the rota for each location by day, by week or by four-week period.

### Shiftwell Mobile

Shiftwell Mobile is the staff app for iOS and Android. Shiftwell Mobile requires iOS 16 or later or Android 11 or later. Staff use Shiftwell Mobile to see their shifts, pick up open shifts, request swaps and clock in.

### Shiftwell Time Clock

Shiftwell Time Clock is a kiosk mode for clocking in and out at a shared device on site. Shiftwell Time Clock runs on an Android tablet with Android 11 or later (or iPad equivalent). Shiftwell Time Clock accepts a 4-digit staff PIN or an NFC staff badge.

## Deployment models

Shiftwell is offered in two deployment models: Shiftwell Cloud and Shiftwell On-Premises.

In the Shiftwell Cloud deployment model, Brightline Software hosts and operates the service. In the Shiftwell On-Premises deployment model, the customer hosts the service on a server inside its own network. Shiftwell On-Premises is available only on the Enterprise plan. Shiftwell On-Premises requires a Linux server with at least 4 CPU cores and 16 GB of RAM (or virtual machine equivalent). Shiftwell On-Premises receives software updates from Brightline Software once per quarter.

## Plans

Shiftwell is sold on two plans: Standard and Enterprise. The Standard plan includes Shiftwell Web, Shiftwell Mobile, rotas, open shifts, shift swaps and timesheets. The Enterprise plan adds single sign-on, the Shiftwell API, Auto-Fill Scheduling, the uptime service level agreement, Shiftwell On-Premises and priority support. Plan details are provided by the Brightline Software account team.

## Offline use

Shiftwell Mobile stores the next 14 days of a staff member's shifts on the phone. Shiftwell Mobile keeps showing the last synced rota when the phone is offline. Shift swap requests made offline are queued and sent when the phone is back online. Picking up an open shift requires an internet connection.

## Unfilled-shift results

In a 2025 pilot across 18 outpatient clinics, locations using Shiftwell reduced unfilled shifts by an average of 27% compared with the same months of the prior year. Unfilled-shift results depend on staff numbers, local labour markets, sickness rates and how open shifts are offered. Brightline Software does not guarantee a specific reduction in unfilled shifts.
