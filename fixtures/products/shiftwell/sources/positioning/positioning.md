## Positioning statement
For managers who run outpatient clinics and care homes on spreadsheets, paper rotas and group texts, Shiftwell is a staff-scheduling service that turns sick calls and open shifts into a quick, fair, phone-first process. Unlike general-purpose rostering software, Shiftwell understands roles, qualifications and safe staffing on every shift, and it is simple enough that frontline staff actually use it.
## Category
Staff scheduling and shift management for clinics and care homes
## Competitive alternatives
- Spreadsheets and paper rotas: free and familiar, but out of date the moment someone swaps a shift, and invisible to staff who are not in the building.
- General-purpose rostering software: flexible, but built for retail and hospitality, with no idea of clinical qualifications or minimum staffing per role.
- Doing nothing (ringing round and group texts): no new cost, but managers lose hours every week chasing cover and agency spend keeps rising.
## Value themes
### Cover in minutes, not mornings
Fill the gap from a sick call before the shift starts.
- Proof: A sick call turns the staff member's shift into an open shift.
- Proof: Push notifications for open shifts reach staff phones in under 5 seconds at the median.
- Proof: Every open shift is filled within 15 minutes.
### Safe staffing, built in
Never publish a rota that leaves a shift short or puts the wrong person on it.
- Proof: Administrators can set a minimum number of staff for each role and each shift.
- Proof: Shiftwell does not assign a shift to a staff member whose required qualification has expired.
### A rota your team actually reads
One version of the rota, on every phone.
- Proof: Shiftwell Mobile keeps showing the last synced rota when the phone is offline.
- Proof: Staff always see the latest rota, wherever they are.
### Trusted by care providers
A choice that IT, data protection and procurement teams sign off quickly.
- Proof: Used by more than 1,200 clinics and care homes across the UK, Ireland and the US.
- Proof: ISO 27001 and HITRUST certified.
- Proof: SOC 2 Type II report available on request.
## Personas
### Care-home manager
- Role: Runs day-to-day staffing for one care home or a small group of homes
- Pains: Early-morning sick calls; ringing round for cover; agency staff filling gaps at short notice
- Goals: Cover shifts fast; keep safe staffing on every shift; spend less on agency staff
- Stage: problem-aware
### Clinic rota coordinator
- Role: Builds and maintains rotas for one or more outpatient clinics
- Pains: Rotas rebuilt by hand every month; swaps made without telling anyone; checking qualifications in a separate spreadsheet
- Goals: Build rotas from templates; a clear record of who changed what; qualifications checked before a shift is assigned
- Stage: solution-aware
### Head of operations
- Role: Owns staffing outcomes and budget across a group of clinics or care homes
- Pains: Agency spend rising; no consistent view of staffing across sites; vendor security reviews that drag on for months
- Goals: Fewer unfilled shifts; one view across every location; a vendor that passes security and data protection review
- Stage: product-aware
## Objections
- My staff will not use another app => Show how little the staff side asks of them; emphasize the calendar feed and one-tap open shifts.
- Our carers have old phones => Explain supported iOS and Android versions and the shared Time Clock tablet option.
- What happens if the system goes down at night => Explain the last synced rota on phones and the offline Time Clock.
- How much does it cost => Route to the account team; do not state prices in copy.
- Who else like us uses it => Route to the account team; do not name customers in copy.
## Awareness stages
- unaware: Lead with the hidden cost of ringing round for cover and last-minute agency shifts, not the product.
- problem-aware: Name the 6am sick call and show that offering the open shift to everyone eligible at once fixes it.
- solution-aware: Compare against spreadsheets and general-purpose rostering software on qualifications, safe staffing and staff adoption.
- product-aware: Answer the top objections: old phones, outages, security and data protection review.
- most-aware: Make the next step easy with a demo using the customer's own rota and clear plan options.
## Messaging
- Headline: Fill every gap in your rota, without ringing round
- Tagline: Never miss a shift.
- Elevator pitch: Shiftwell is staff scheduling for clinics and care homes. Managers build the rota in Shiftwell Web, staff see it in Shiftwell Mobile, and when someone calls in sick the shift goes straight to everyone who is free and qualified to cover it.
