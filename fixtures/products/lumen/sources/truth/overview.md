# Lumen Hub overview

Lumen Hub is a building climate controller made by Northwind Devices. Lumen Hub combines a wall-mounted controller, wireless sensors and the Lumen app. Lumen Hub controls heating, ventilation and air conditioning (HVAC) equipment in commercial buildings.

## Who Lumen Hub is for

Lumen Hub is designed for commercial buildings between 500 and 20,000 square meters of floor area. Typical Lumen Hub users are facilities managers, building engineers and property operators. Lumen Hub is used in offices, schools, clinics and retail stores.

A single Lumen Hub controls up to 16 HVAC zones. Multiple Lumen Hubs can be grouped into one site in the Lumen app. A Lumen site can contain up to 50 Lumen Hubs. An organization in the Lumen app can contain an unlimited number of sites.

## Components

### The Hub

The Lumen Hub controller mounts on a standard single-gang electrical back box. The Lumen Hub controller connects to HVAC equipment through 24 VAC relay outputs or through BACnet/IP. The Lumen Hub controller has a 3.5-inch color touchscreen for on-site adjustments.

### Sensors

Lumen Sensors measure temperature, relative humidity and carbon dioxide (CO2). Lumen Sensors are battery powered and mount on a wall or ceiling without wiring. Each Lumen Hub supports up to 32 wireless sensors.

### The Lumen app

The Lumen app is available for iOS, Android and web browsers. The Lumen app shows live readings, schedules, alerts and energy reports for every zone. Changes made in the Lumen app are synchronized to the Hub over the internet.

## Deployment models

Lumen Hub is offered in two deployment models: Lumen Cloud and Lumen Edge Server.

In the Lumen Cloud deployment model, Northwind Devices hosts and operates the management service. In the Lumen Edge Server deployment model, the customer hosts the management service on a server inside the building network. Lumen Edge Server is available only on the Enterprise plan. Lumen Edge Server requires a Linux server with at least 4 CPU cores and 8 GB of RAM (or virtual machine equivalent). Lumen Edge Server receives software updates from Northwind Devices once per quarter.

## Plans

Lumen Hub is sold on two plans: Standard and Enterprise. The Standard plan includes the Lumen app, schedules, alerts and energy reports. The Enterprise plan adds single sign-on, the Lumen API, the uptime service level agreement, Lumen Edge Server and priority support. Plan details are provided by the Northwind Devices account team.

## Offline operation

Lumen Hub runs heating and cooling schedules locally on the device. Lumen Hub keeps running its last synchronized schedule during an internet outage. On-site adjustments on the Lumen Hub touchscreen continue to work during an internet outage. Remote changes from the Lumen app are not possible until the internet connection returns.

## Energy results

In a 2025 pilot across 12 office buildings, sites using Lumen Hub reduced HVAC energy use by an average of 18% compared with the same months of the prior year. Energy results depend on climate, building envelope, equipment age and occupancy patterns. Northwind Devices does not guarantee a specific energy saving.
