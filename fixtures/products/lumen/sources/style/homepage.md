# Comfortable buildings, without the guesswork

You look after a building. People count on you to keep it warm in winter and cool in summer. Lumen Hub helps you do that with less effort.

Lumen Hub is one small controller on the wall. It talks to your heating and cooling. It listens to wireless sensors in every room. And it puts all of it in one app you can open anywhere.

[See how Lumen Hub works]

## Your building, in one view

You shouldn't have to walk the halls to know how a room feels. With Lumen Hub, you don't.

Open the Lumen app and you see every zone at a glance. Temperature. Humidity. Air quality. If something drifts, you know first.

- See every room, live
- Fix problems from your phone
- Get alerts before people complain

## Set it once. Then relax.

Schedules take minutes, not afternoons. Pick the hours. Pick the comfort range. Lumen Hub does the rest, day after day.

You can lock in limits, too. People can still tap the screen to warm up a room. They just can't push it past the range you set. That keeps everyone comfortable and keeps your energy use in check.

## Keeps working when the internet doesn't

Internet goes down? Your building stays comfortable. Lumen Hub runs its schedule right on the device. When the connection comes back, Lumen Hub catches up on its own.

## Works with what you already have

You don't need to rip anything out. Lumen Hub connects to the heating and cooling you already run. Your installer maps it all in one visit.

- Connects to your existing equipment
- Installs in about an afternoon
- Grows with you, one zone at a time

## Built to be trusted

Your building data matters. We treat it that way.

Lumen Hub only runs software we have signed. Your data is encrypted on its way to the cloud and while it sits there. And you decide who can change what, with simple roles for your team.

## Questions you might have

### Do I need new heating and cooling equipment?

No. Lumen Hub works with the equipment you already have. Your installer checks it during the first visit.

### Who installs Lumen Hub?

A certified installer does. They wire the Hub, pair the sensors and set up your zones. You get a building that works on day one.

### Can my whole team use it?

Yes. Invite as many people as you like. Give each person the access they need, and nothing more.
