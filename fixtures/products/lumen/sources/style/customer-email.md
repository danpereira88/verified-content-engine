Subject: Welcome to Lumen Hub. Here's your first week.

Hi there,

Welcome to Lumen Hub. We're glad you're here.

Your installer has finished, and your building is now running on Lumen Hub. You don't have to do anything for it to keep working. But a few small steps this week will help you get the most out of it.

Here's what we suggest.

## Day 1: Open the app and look around

Sign in to the Lumen app. You'll see each zone in your building, with live readings.

Take a minute to check that the zone names make sense to you. If "Zone 3" should really be "Front office", rename it. It makes every alert easier to read later.

## Day 2: Set your schedules

Schedules are where Lumen Hub starts to save you time.

Pick a zone. Choose the hours people are in. Choose the comfort range you want during those hours. Then copy the schedule to similar zones in two taps.

- Start with your busiest zones
- Keep comfort ranges simple at first
- Adjust after a week of real use

## Day 3: Invite your team

You don't have to do this alone. Invite the people who help you run the building.

Give each person a role. Viewers can see. Operators can change schedules. Administrators can manage everything. You can change a role at any time.

## Day 4: Turn on the alerts you care about

Lumen Hub can tell you when a room gets too warm, too cold or too stuffy. It can tell you when a sensor battery is low.

Turn on the alerts you want. Turn off the ones you don't. You're in charge of your inbox.

## Day 5: Look at your first report

By now, Lumen Hub has a few days of activity. Open the reports tab and pick this week.

You'll see when your equipment ran and for how long. It's a good first look at where your energy goes.

## Need a hand?

We're here. Reply to this email, or visit our help center. Real people read every message.

A few things people often ask in week one:

- How do I rename a zone? Open the zone, tap the name, type the new one.
- How do I stop people turning the heat way up? Set a comfort range for the zone.
- What if the internet goes down? Lumen Hub keeps running your schedule. You don't need to do anything.

## One last thing

Lumen Hub works best when you let it settle in. Give it a week. Make small changes. Watch what happens.

We think you'll like what you see.

Warmly,

The Lumen Hub team at Northwind Devices
