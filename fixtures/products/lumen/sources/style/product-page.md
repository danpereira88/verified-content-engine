# Meet Lumen Hub

Lumen Hub is the easy way to run heating and cooling in your building. It's a controller, a set of wireless sensors and an app. Together, they keep your people comfortable and your bills down.

[Book a walkthrough]

## The controller

The Lumen Hub controller sits on the wall like any other control. It has a bright touchscreen, so anyone can make a quick change on the spot. Behind it, Lumen Hub talks to your heating and cooling equipment.

- A clear touchscreen for quick changes
- Fits a standard wall box
- Runs your schedule on its own

You don't have to think about it much. That's the point.

## The sensors

Lumen Sensors go where people are. Stick one on a wall or a ceiling. No wires. No drilling into ducts.

Each sensor checks temperature, humidity and air quality. It sends readings to Lumen Hub every few minutes. The batteries last for years, and the app tells you when one is running low.

- No wiring needed
- Reads temperature, humidity and air quality
- Long battery life, with a heads-up before it runs out

## The Lumen app

The Lumen app is where it all comes together. Open it on your phone, your tablet or your laptop.

You see every zone in every building you manage. You change a schedule in a few taps. You get an alert when a room gets too warm, too cold or too stuffy.

- Every building, one app
- Schedules in a few taps
- Alerts that actually help

## Made for the people who run buildings

You know your building better than anyone. Lumen Hub doesn't try to replace that. It just gives you better eyes and a faster way to act.

Facilities managers use Lumen Hub to cut down on comfort complaints. Building engineers use it to spot equipment trouble early. Property teams use it to see all their sites in one place.

## Comfort with guardrails

Here's a common story. Someone feels cold, turns the heat way up, and forgets about it. The room bakes all afternoon.

With Lumen Hub, you set a comfort range for each zone. People can still make a room warmer or cooler. They just stay inside the range. You keep control without saying no to anyone.

## Your team, your rules

Not everyone needs the same access. With Lumen Hub, you give each person a role.

- Viewers can see readings and reports
- Operators can change schedules and temperatures
- Administrators manage people and settings

Simple to set up. Easy to change later.

## Reports you can share

Lumen Hub turns equipment activity into clear energy reports. Pick a day, a week or a month. Download a file and send it to your manager or your finance team.

## Plays well with others

Lumen Hub connects with the building systems you already use. If you have a building management system, Lumen Hub can work alongside it. If you want to connect your own tools, there's an API on the Enterprise plan.
