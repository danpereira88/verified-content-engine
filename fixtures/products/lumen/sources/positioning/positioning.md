## Positioning statement
For facilities teams who run small and mid-sized commercial buildings without a full building management system, Lumen Hub is a building climate controller that gives them live, room-level control of heating and cooling from one app. Unlike a traditional building management system, Lumen Hub installs in a single visit, works with the equipment already in place, and is simple enough for the whole facilities team to use every day.
## Category
Building climate control for small and mid-sized commercial buildings
## Competitive alternatives
- Programmable thermostats: cheap and familiar, but no room-level visibility, no central view across buildings and no alerts.
- Traditional building management system (BMS): powerful and deeply integrated, but expensive, slow to install and usually needs a specialist contractor for every change.
- Doing nothing (manual walk-arounds and complaint emails): no new cost, but the team spends hours reacting to comfort complaints and wastes energy heating empty rooms.
## Value themes
### See every room
Know how every room feels, live, before anyone complains.
- Proof: Lumen Sensors measure temperature, relative humidity and CO2 in each room.
- Proof: The Lumen app sends alerts for out-of-range temperature and high CO2.
### Comfort with guardrails
Let people adjust their space without letting a single room run away.
- Proof: Administrators can set minimum and maximum setpoint limits for each zone.
- Proof: The change approval policy requires a second Administrator to approve configuration changes.
### Keeps running, no matter what
The building stays comfortable even when the network does not.
- Proof: Lumen Hub keeps running its last synchronized schedule during an internet outage.
- Proof: Lumen Hub has never lost a sensor reading at any customer site.
### Trusted by facilities teams
A proven choice that security and procurement teams sign off quickly.
- Proof: Trusted by more than 500 buildings across Europe and North America.
- Proof: ISO 27001 certified hardware and cloud.
- Proof: SOC 2 Type II report available on request.
## Personas
### Facilities manager
- Role: Runs day-to-day operations for one or more commercial buildings
- Pains: Constant comfort complaints; no visibility into rooms without walking the floor; heating schedules nobody remembers setting
- Goals: Fewer complaints; one view across all sites; prove energy savings to finance
- Stage: problem-aware
### Building engineer
- Role: Maintains HVAC equipment and building controls
- Pains: Integrating new controls with older equipment; changes made by people who should not make them; finding faults late
- Goals: Works with existing BACnet equipment; clear audit trail of changes; early warning of equipment faults
- Stage: solution-aware
### Property portfolio director
- Role: Owns budget and operating outcomes across a portfolio of buildings
- Pains: Energy costs rising; no consistent data across buildings; vendor security reviews take months
- Goals: Lower energy spend; consistent reporting; a vendor that passes security review
- Stage: product-aware
## Objections
- It will be another system my team does not use => Show the app in the persona's own building terms; emphasize roles and the short first week.
- Our equipment is too old => Explain BACnet and relay connections; offer an installer site check.
- What happens if the internet goes down => Explain local schedules and buffered readings.
- How much does it cost => Route to the account team; do not state prices in copy.
- Who else like us uses it => Route to the account team; do not name customers in copy.
## Awareness stages
- unaware: Lead with the cost of comfort complaints and wasted heating hours, not the product.
- problem-aware: Name the pain of reacting to complaints and show that room-level visibility fixes it.
- solution-aware: Compare against programmable thermostats and a traditional BMS on install effort and day-to-day use.
- product-aware: Answer the top objections: old equipment, outages, security review.
- most-aware: Make the next step easy with a walkthrough booking and clear plan options.
## Messaging
- Headline: Comfortable buildings, without the guesswork
- Tagline: Never lose a reading.
- Elevator pitch: Lumen Hub is one controller, wireless sensors and an app that give facilities teams live, room-level control of heating and cooling, with guardrails that keep every room in range and a schedule that keeps running even when the internet does not.
