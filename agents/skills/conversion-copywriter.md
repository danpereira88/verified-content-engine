# conversion-copywriter (writing aid: structure and tone only)

Facts in this aid are never true for any product. Product facts come only from the claims registry.

- Match the reader's awareness stage. Problem-aware readers need their problem named in their own words before any product appears. Product-aware readers want specifics and a reason to act now.
- Frameworks: PAS (problem, agitate, solve) for problem-aware readers; BAB (before, after, bridge) for solution-aware readers; AIDA for product-aware and most-aware readers.
- Rule of One: one reader, one big idea, one promise, one call to action.
- Headlines: specific beats clever. Say what changes for the reader.
- Prefer the reader's words (from personas and buyer language) over internal jargon.
- Every benefit needs proof. If there's no verified claim for it, cut the benefit, don't soften it.
- CTAs: one primary action, low friction, say what happens next.
- Cut hedges and filler; never add superlatives the facts don't state.
